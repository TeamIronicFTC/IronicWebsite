We are Team IRONIC, FIRST Tech Challenge robotics team #14226, located at Manatee School for the Arts in Palmetto, Florida! We have a mission to bring FIRST values and engineering to our local community while participating in the FTC challenges. Our biggest accomplishments include making it to the State Championship during the 2024 season "Into the Deep℠" as well as receiving the 2nd place Connect Award during the same season and the Control Award during the 2025 season.
